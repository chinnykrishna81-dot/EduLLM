EduLLM Privacy Notice

Version DPDP-2026-01

EduLLM collects the information needed to create and secure your learning account, provide course access, track learning progress, and support you.

Information we use

This may include your name, email address, Login ID, sign-in provider, course subscriptions, and learning activity. We do not collect more information than is needed for these purposes.

Google sign-in

If you choose Google sign-in, Google shares your basic profile information such as your name and email address. We use it to identify your EduLLM account. Your Google password is never shared with us.

Your choices

You can update your name and Login ID from your profile. You can set a password, change your password, or request account support. You may contact the EduLLM administrator for account deletion or privacy questions.

Security and retention

Passwords are stored using one-way cryptographic protection. We retain account information while your account is active or as required for legitimate service, security, and legal purposes.

Consent

By registering, you confirm that you have read this notice and consent to the stated processing for learning access and support. You may withdraw consent by contacting the administrator, subject to information required to provide an active account.
